## 1. Summary of the record

> Fictional example for layout testing. Unless marked otherwise, statements below come from D01, the investigators' own peer-reviewed trial report.

The Example Hospital investigators report that 4,812 patients were randomised in the ExampleAI trial `[F004]`. The median time from arrival to first clinical assessment was 38 minutes with ExampleAI queue ordering and 52 minutes with usual triage `[F008]`, a difference of 14 minutes `[F017]`. Whether any serious adverse event was attributable to ExampleAI is disputed in the record `[F010]`.

## 2. Parties and roles

The trial registry lists Example Hospital as the sponsor and ExampleAI Ltd as a collaborator `[F001]`.

## 3. The system and its configuration

In the intervention arm, ExampleAI ordered the waiting queue, and triage nurses could override the order at any time `[F002]`. ExampleAI Ltd reports that ExampleAI is used in 40 hospitals `[F012]`.

## 4. Conditions before the event

The library has found no description in D01 to D04 of how the emergency department ordered its waiting queue before the trial `[F015]`.

## 6. How the facts were established

The trial was registered on 10 January 2025, according to the registry record `[F003]`. The investigators report that enrolment ran from 1 February 2025 to 31 July 2025 `[F013]`. They report that 4,812 patients were randomised `[F004]`, of whom 2,401 were allocated to ExampleAI queue ordering `[F005]`, which leaves 2,411 allocated to usual triage `[F006]`. The library has found no statement in D01 of how many patients had no recorded time of first assessment `[F007]`.

## 8. Outcomes and measured effects

The median time from arrival to first clinical assessment was 38 minutes with ExampleAI queue ordering and 52 minutes with usual triage `[F008]`, a difference of 14 minutes `[F017]`. The investigators report under-triage in 3.1 percent of encounters with ExampleAI and 3.0 percent with usual triage `[F009]`. The investigators report that no serious adverse events were attributed to ExampleAI; the Example University audit group reported two in which the ExampleAI queue position was recorded as a contributing factor `[F010]`.

## 9. Statements by the parties

ExampleAI Ltd stated in a press release that "ExampleAI cuts emergency waiting times in half" `[F011]`.

## 10. Discrepancies in the record

The investigators report that enrolment ran to 31 July 2025 `[F013]`, and the registry lists the overall end date as 30 June 2025 `[F014]`. The count of serious adverse events attributed to ExampleAI is disputed between D01 and D04 `[F010]`.

## 11. What the record does not establish

The library has found no statement in D01 to D04 of what ExampleAI cost Example Hospital `[F016]`.
