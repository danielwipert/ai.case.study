The Fortune 500 support study found gains concentrated in novices and little for experts — a gradient that falls monotonically with skill. The randomized replication at another firm found an inverted U: mid-tier agents gained most on speed, and the top quintile got measurably *worse* on customer ratings and on repeat contacts alike.

Both are in the same case record. Neither has been reconciled, and "AI helps the least experienced most" is the version that travels.
